# Stacklink — Round One Ventures pitch

A 5-slide investor-style pitch built for the Round One Ventures workshop. Stacklink as the "Sovereign Knowledge OS" for regulated EU mid-market companies. Two-student team, not raising. The asks are: people to talk to, honest feedback, stay in touch.

This is the canonical `stacklink-dark` reference deck. Use it when you want a pitch with the Stacklink visual language: dark background, restrained sans-serif, EU-enterprise tone, connector chips, kpi tiles.

## Stage rhythm (5 slides)

1. Hero — wordmark + tagline ("the company brain")
2. Problem — fragmented enterprise knowledge, real-customer quote
3. Solution — connector chips converging into the brain
4. Proof — KPI tiles + traction
5. Ask — three asks with explicit "we're not raising"
